Pet Care FAQ

Before the first visit.

A few things to talk through before you and your pet meet Myomi.

Let’s talk details.

What should I share before a visit?

Have your pet’s routine, feeding schedule, triggers, and behavior around other animals handy. Talk through any special needs before confirming care.

What should we agree on for an emergency?

Before confirming care, agree on how to reach you, who can respond on your behalf, and which vet to list in the care plan.

How do we arrange access to my home?

Agree with Myomi on how to hand over and return keys. Share access codes directly through the private channel you agree to use.

What should I confirm before booking?

Confirm the total price, visit length, availability, and the terms for cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds.
